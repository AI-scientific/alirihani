Ali Rihani, PhD

Senior Research Infrastructure Specialist, Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology (MTC)

I have more than 15 years of research experience in cancer biology and qPCR, and during the COVID-19 pandemic I helped build and run high-throughput diagnostic, sample-processing and biobanking pipelines. My work focuses on developing molecular methods, especially multiplex qPCR and droplet digital PCR (ddPCR), for large-scale diagnostic and surveillance applications.

Current work

At the Department of Microbiology, Tumor and Cell Biology, I develop diagnostic methods for high-throughput pipelines and platforms, and I run research projects with external collaborators.

High-multiplex ddPCR

Designing and optimising multiplex probe panels for droplet digital PCR assays that detect viral pathogens and antimicrobial-resistance genes.

Environmental surveillance

Air sampling combined with six-plex ddPCR to track respiratory viruses in settings such as elderly-care homes.

Mucosal immunity studies

Laboratory support for collaborative clinical studies of mucosal IgA and lower-airway antibodies, including antibody measurement in exhaled breath particles.

Lab automation & LIMS

Scripting automated liquid handlers (Opentrons, MGI, BRAND) and building LIMS tools for sample traceability, analysis and reporting.

Teaching

Lab teaching on the Molecular Medicine – Oncology course (OncoPath and MTC, Karolinska Institutet) since September 2023.
